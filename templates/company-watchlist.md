# Company Watchlist

> Companies you want to track for future openings, even if nothing is posted right now.
> The discovery skill scans each company's career page directly on every run.

## How to Use
- Add companies below with a brief note on why they're interesting
- The discovery skill will check their career pages and flag any matches with `[WATCHLIST]`
- To remove a company, delete its entry or move it to the Retired section
